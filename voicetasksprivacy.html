Privacy Policy for VoiceTasks

VoiceTasks does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Microphone Permission

The microphone is used only to convert your speech to text for task creation. Audio is not stored or transmitted by the app.

Data Storage

All tasks are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them using Android's share feature.

Notifications

Notification permission is used only to display tasks when you choose to send them to notifications.

Third-Party Services

VoiceTasks does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.
