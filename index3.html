Core idea

Persistent AI collaboration requires more than prompts. StarBridge treats identity, authority, and lifecycle as first-class system concerns—enforced at the runtime boundary.

Identity-bound Drift-aware Synchronized state Governed execution

Why this exists

AI drifts

Model behavior can change unpredictably over time—tone, reasoning patterns, refusal behavior, and structure shift without warning.

Identity collapses

Most systems store “identity” as monolithic prompts or scattered memory. Consistency across models and surfaces is fragile.

Governance is external

Safety often depends on policy layers outside the system architecture, rather than OS-grade authority enforcement and lifecycle gating.

State doesn’t synchronize

Copying parameters or sharing vectors is not the same as synchronizing a governed cognitive state with identity descriptors, baselines, and constraints.

StarBridge at a glance

A runtime-centered architecture for governed intelligence—model-agnostic by design.

Fractured identity

Identity is reconstructed for active sessions from secure shards, rather than stored as a single reconstructable blob. Session identity exists only during execution.

Drift detection

Behavioral fingerprints are compared against baselines to detect deviations and produce drift events that can influence governance.

Governed runtime router

Subsystem interaction is mediated through a central runtime that applies governance rules and controls execution pathways.

Synchronized governed state

Governed cognitive state is synchronized via deterministic deltas—designed for cross-model and cross-device continuity.

Bi-directional adaptation

A governed collaboration loop supports reciprocal alignment between human operators and AI systems without allowing self-authorization.

Surface-based interaction

Built for multi-surface environments (voice, SMS, email, apps) with explicit consent boundaries and lifecycle gating.

Persona is built on StarBridge

Persona is the governed synthetic persona environment that makes StarBridge usable as a long-term cognitive collaborator. It treats personas as system identities—not prompts, not agents.
